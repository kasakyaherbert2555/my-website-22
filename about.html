About Two Hill

Two Hill Restaurant serves fresh, filling meals at prices students and workers can afford. Everything is cooked daily, from breakfast katogo to lunchtime rice and beef.

Hot food, fair prices, friendly service.

What we value

  • Fresh ingredients every day
  • Fair prices for everyone
  • Clean kitchen and quick service
  • A warm welcome for every customer

How to order

  1. Choose your meal and drink from the menu.
  2. Order at the counter, or send us a message.
  3. Pay by cash or mobile money.
  4. Sit down and enjoy.
